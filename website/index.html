Android keyboard

Switch to the Memlib keyboard in any text field. Tap to insert, or long-press to open the share sheet when an app does not accept images.

Optional online features

More ways to find and use media

Memlib can search GIPHY and display results with attribution. Saving GIPHY results to your library is disabled until provider approval is granted.

A Pinterest connection is being evaluated and is not available in the current app. Any future feature will follow Pinterest's authorization and data-use requirements.
